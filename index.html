ACC Squamish Moving Forward

Trips

One of the most basic and most fundamental reasons people join are trips. Want to join or lead trips? We've got you covered! Check out our upcoming events on Groups Place.

Social Events

Learn about trips that people have embarked on across the country and leave with a full cup of motivation to get outside yourself. Stay tuned on Facebook for information about our next get-together.

Courses

Join us and learn the basics of mountain travel from our experienced club volunteers, or sign up for a guided course. We have plans for both!

Equipment

Don't want to buy an inReach for two weekends per year? Neither would we! We have not-so-distant gear sharing plans in the works for ACC members, so you no longer have to skimp on snow pickets or ice-screws!
